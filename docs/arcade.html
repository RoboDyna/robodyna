RoboDyna Arcade

Can you beat the robots?

One browser game for each of the 30 RoboDyna tasks, played with the same point-and-click and keyboard controls our human participants used. Each game is 5 episodes; at the end you see your success rate next to π0.5, X-VLA and FastWAM on the same task. Physics here is a simplified 2D take on the SAPIEN scenes.
